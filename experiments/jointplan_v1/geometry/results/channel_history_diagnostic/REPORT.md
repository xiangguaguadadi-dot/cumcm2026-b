## Completed public-history finding

All **96/96** detached P0-derived public prefixes have exactly one distinct
actual no_signal coordinate set among their unknown channels: 48/48 for Q3 and
48/48 for Q4. Each remaining station is also assigned the same complete set of
unknown channels. None has channel-specific history diversity. Q3 has 6–19
unknown channels per prefix, Q4 9–19. Input SHA256 and every prefix's counts are
in `history_groups.json`; the computation used no future response or score.

For this snapshot contract, each unknown channel therefore faces the same
continuous coverage obligation and identical future station choices. Deleting
one station's measure is feasible for one such channel exactly when the same
geometric deletion is feasible for the others. Thus the implemented paid
channel pruning currently reduces to repeating the same geometric station
cover, rather than exploiting distinct per-channel histories. This explains
why the intended independent assignment freedom was absent. It is not, by
itself, proof that no station can be removed or that no better layout exists.
The frozen batch found zero certified paid-channel deletions.

A legal way to create genuine differences would be to pay for measurement of
only a strict subset of unknown channels at an actual reached point, or to
commit a complete future plan assigning different channel subsets to different
real points. Each unmeasured channel must retain its own full certified future
coverage; passing a point or measuring another channel supplies no evidence.
The additional action pays 5 s plus any 1 s channel switch and actual movement.
A known service path point is only free of incremental movement if the robot
actually reaches that exact point. Such asymmetric sensing would be a changed
action mechanism and still needs full conditional costs and certificates. Its
possible usefulness is unproved, and the older paid opportunistic scans already
have negative evidence. No experiment on it is being proposed for automatic
continuation after the stop instruction.
